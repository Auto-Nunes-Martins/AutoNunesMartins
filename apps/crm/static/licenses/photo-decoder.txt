Local HEIC/HEIF photo decoder

This CRM uses heic-to 1.6.7 by Hopper Gee, unmodified, loaded separately
only when native HEIC decoding is unavailable. It includes libheif 1.23.7
(struktur AG and contributors) and libde265 (struktur AG and contributors).
These libraries are distributed under LGPL version 3 or later, without warranty.
The application permits reverse engineering for debugging modifications to
these LGPL libraries. You may replace or modify these libraries under their
licence; rebuild the CRM with the replacement package using its normal Vite build.

Full licence and notices: heic-to-LICENSE.txt and GPL-3.0.txt (in this directory).
Exact distributed package, including source, embedded decoder and build scripts:
https://registry.npmjs.org/heic-to/-/heic-to-1.6.7.tgz
Wrapper source: https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to/tree/v1.6.7
Decoder source: https://github.com/strukturag/libheif/tree/v1.23.7
HEVC decoder source: https://github.com/strukturag/libde265/tree/v1.0.16
Build instructions: https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to#development-guide

Only the first/primary image of a HEIC/HEIF container is used, not motion,
depth or auxiliary images. Conversion runs on the user's device in an isolated
worker, one photograph at a time. Original suitable JPEG/PNG/WebP files are
not re-encoded locally; the server performs privacy sanitisation and final
optimisation for all accepted images. Prepared canvas output contains no EXIF.
