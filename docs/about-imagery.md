# Quem Somos — imagens de demonstração

As duas imagens abaixo foram criadas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, em 21 de setembro de 2026. São representações fictícias: não documentam as instalações, trabalhadores ou serviços reais da Auto Nunes Martins. Os retratos existentes continuam a ser fotografias ilustrativas. Substituir por imagens confirmadas antes da publicação.

A composição, os textos e as animações da página foram preservados. Os ficheiros finais são WebP com variantes responsivas, servidos pelo próprio website; não dependem de uma API de imagens em produção.

No ajuste de ritmo pedido posteriormente, a página passou a utilizar os tokens de largura e espaçamento do Orbit. O separador fotográfico experimental foi substituído por uma pausa tipográfica que reutiliza `OrbitPerspective`, o mesmo componente de iluminação progressiva da homepage. A frase «A confiança começa nas pessoas que o acompanham.» faz a transição da preparação das viaturas para a equipa. Os valores por omissão do componente preservam a frase original da homepage.

## Prompt final — stand

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: photographic hero for the About page of an independent Portuguese used-car dealership, a clearly illustrative prototype, not real premises. Generate one landscape 3:2 editorial photograph of a welcoming, believable small modern independent car showroom seen from just outside its open glazed entrance. Four or five ordinary well-kept European hatchbacks, an estate car and a compact SUV in charcoal, silver and warm white, parked in a neat diagonal with generous walking space. Modest single-storey architecture, white plaster, dark slender metal window frames, grey concrete floor, a small wooden reception desk visible at the back, subtle potted greenery. Camera eye level with natural 35mm perspective, architectural lines straight, front car and entrance both fully readable in the central 75 percent for responsive crops. Bright soft natural late-morning daylight, warm neutral colour, balanced contrast, realistic reflections and mild everyday imperfections, quiet and professional rather than opulent. The building and the variety of cars are the subject, not a single hero sports car. No people necessary. No readable text, no signage, no names, no logos, no recognisable dealership branding, no visible car badges, no watermarks. No supercars, no racing aesthetics, no dramatic red lighting, no luxurious futuristic showroom, no CGI or architectural-render look. Crisp high-quality natural photograph.

## Prompt final — preparação

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: illustrative photograph for the section explaining careful preparation at a small independent Portuguese used-car showroom. Generate a single square editorial photograph, natural documentary realism. Close three-quarter view of a well-kept dark graphite everyday European estate car being carefully prepared in a bright modest showroom. The visible torso and forearms of one adult staff member in a plain navy work shirt, gently wiping the hood near the front headlight with a folded grey microfiber cloth. Hands naturally shaped and proportioned. The staff member's face stays outside the frame, the subject is the attentive gesture and clean paintwork, not an identifiable employee. A believable white plaster wall, glazed entrance and one softly out-of-focus silver car in the background, subtle warm wooden reception detail. Soft natural late-morning daylight, neutral grey and warm-white colour grade, realistic imperfect reflections, no artificial gloss. Composition: main hand, cloth and headlight in the central 65 percent so a near-square responsive crop retains the gesture. Leave a little breathing room around the subject. No visible logos, no readable signage, no badges, no typography, no watermark. No luxurious supercars, racing aesthetics, dramatic lighting or CGI. This is a fictional demonstrative showroom image, not documentary evidence of a real business.
